# THE INFINITY AI BUILDFEST 2026 — Official Knowledge Base
*Build Locally. Lead Globally.*

**Event date:** Friday, June 12, 2026, 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM, BRAC University (Merul Badda Campus), Dhaka.
**Register:** https://cloudcampbd.com/the-infinity-ai-buildfest
**Participant's Guide:** https://bit.ly/infinity-buildfest
**Facebook Event:** https://www.facebook.com/share/1UudeVi2Zt/
**Organizer:** CloudCamp Bangladesh · **Co-Organizer:** BRAC University

## 1. Vision & Strategic Intent

A structured, AI-native execution ecosystem (not a hackathon, not a classroom
competition) where students, developers, and Non-Resident Bangladeshi (NRB)
experts collaborate to build deployable, production-grade AI systems.
Strategic goal: position Bangladesh as a trusted source of AI-native
builders for the global economy.

## 2. Innovation Tracks (choose exactly ONE)

Participants may propose their own idea instead, but self-chosen ideas are
not eligible for awards (may still be showcased).

| Track | Focus | Required elements |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Education (EdTech)** | Adaptive AI tutors, assessments, teacher copilots, career engines, multilingual systems | Personalization logic, curriculum alignment, impact measurement, accessibility |
| **2. Branding & Marketing (MarTech)** | Multimodal brand intelligence, generation, segmentation, digital-twin branding | Multimodal architecture, ROI model, cross-border scalability |
| **3. Healthcare (HealthTech)** | Maternal companions, AI triage, preventive systems, rural telehealth | Ethical safeguards, clinical validation, offline resilience, data protection |
| **4. Online Commerce (E-Commerce)** | Demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, SME dashboards, recommendations | Forecasting logic, business validation, scalability path |
| **5. Social Media (InfoTech)** | Fact-checking, deepfake detection, misinformation filtering, trust scoring | Validation logic, bias detection, real-time monitoring, transparency |

Each track lists 6 specific challenge options (5 defined + 1 "Custom/Showcase")
with a required tech stack, example winning solution, and target KPIs — see
the full per-track tables preserved below in §2b if needed for submission
planning.

### Global Collaboration Layer (all tracks)

Teams may include an NRB expert or international advisor as an active team
member (architecture, product strategy, compliance, market validation,
deployment standards). All NRB contributions must be transparently documented.

## 7. Official Judging Framework (100-point scale)

| Criteria | Weight | What's evaluated |
|---|---|---|
| Innovation | 20% | Originality, non-obvious AI application |
| Technical Execution | 20% | Architecture quality, AI integration depth, robustness |
| Business Model (+ Global Readiness) | 20% | Monetization, adoption path, cross-border viability |
| Real-World Impact (+ Ethical AI Compliance) | 20% | Problem relevance, measurable benefit, responsible AI |
| Scalability (+ NRB Collaboration) | 10% | Deployment feasibility, modular design, global integration |
| Presentation | 10% | Clarity, structure, confidence |

Score bands (e.g. Innovation): 0–5 incremental, 6–10 moderate, 11–15 strong,
16–20 breakthrough/globally competitive. Similar 4-band structure applies to
each 20%-weight category; Scalability and Presentation use 0–3/4–7/8–10 bands.
Projects are judged independently against the rubric, not comparatively.

## 8. Vibe Coding to Production (VCP) — individual challenge

Build a working AI app using **Lovable or Cursor with a maximum of 5
prompts**. Individual only, no teams. Two phases:

- **Preliminary** (May 30 9:00 AM BST – June 3 11:59 PM BST): submit 5
  prompts + optional additional prompts; multi-day, autosaved, revisable.
  AI auto-scores each prompt (0–100) + composite score, then Human-in-the-
  Loop review can accept/adjust/override; qualification via top scores +
  wildcard + special picks.
- **Final** (June 12, on-site at BRAC): strictly **60 minutes**, live,
  synchronized, auto-locks at 00:00:00 (no grace period, whatever exists at
  the deadline is the submission). Must submit Prompt 1–5 + live Lovable
  project URL.

Recommended tools: Lovable.dev (preferred, with credits), Cursor, Claude
Code, Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini (prompt engineering), Supabase, GitHub.

Fair play: original work only, no direct prompt copying; platform runs
semantic/AI similarity and duplicate-prompt detection.

## 9. Opportunity Connect

## 10. Roles & Team Composition

Stakeholder roles: Participants, Mentors (guide only — do NOT write code or
influence judging), Judges (objective, no conflict of interest), Organizers.

Team execution roles (5, one person may cover multiple): Team Leader,
Business Analyst/Data Scientist, UI/UX/Frontend Developer, Backend/Database/
Scraper Engineer, Presentation/Communication Lead.

Prescriptive composition: 2–3 members able to build full-stack AI systems;
≥1 member focused on business/domain; encourage ≥1 female participant per
team (bonus points); cross-institution teams (including polytechnic/madrasah)
encouraged; NRB/diaspora member encouraged with a defined role.

### Top 10 mistakes to avoid
1. Using AI as a gimmick (no real intelligence/impact)
2. No clear problem definition
3. Weak architecture thinking (no input→AI→output flow)
4. Ignoring real data (fake/hardcoded)
5. No personalization
6. No Bangla/localization
7. Overbuilding features instead of one polished use case
8. No scalability thinking (local-machine-only demo)
9. Poor demo & storytelling
10. Not leveraging team diversity (no business/domain/NRB input)
11. (bonus) Not using modern AI tools properly — Lovable, Cursor, Claude Code, MCP, RAG, agents

## 2b. Per-track challenge options, stacks & example "winning formula" builds

Each track offers 5 defined challenges plus a 6th open "Custom/Showcase"
option (must still hit the track's required elements). Full detail below.

### Track 1 — Education (EdTech)
1. Adaptive AI Tutor — personalized pacing/gaps
2. AI Exam Prep Engine — dynamic tests + readiness scoring
3. Remedial Learning Engine — targets weak areas, esp. rural/low-performing
4. Multimodal Learning Assistant — voice/text/image, offline + Bangla voice
5. Learning Analytics Dashboard — insights for teachers/parents
6. Custom (must include personalization + RAG)

Required stack (all mandatory): Lovable + Cursor/Claude Code + LLM + Local
LLM + RAG + GraphDB + Scraper. Resources: NCTB curriculum, Khan Academy.
Example winning build ("ShikkhaAI"): Student → Profiling → RAG (NCTB + past
papers) → LLM reasoning → Adaptive tasks → Analytics → Feedback; Bangla-
first UI + TTS/STT; offline mode via local LLM; teacher copilot. Target
KPIs: +25–40% score improvement in 4 weeks, reduced time-to-mastery,
engagement up.

### Track 2 — Branding & Marketing (MarTech)
1. Multimodal Content Engine (text/image/video/voice)
2. Brand Simulation Engine (campaign outcome prediction, GraphRAG)
3. Audience Intelligence AI (segmentation, GraphDB)
4. Influencer Matching Engine (brand↔creator, trust/authenticity)
5. Campaign Optimization AI (real-time, RL models)
6. Custom (must include ROI logic)

Example ("BrandTwin AI"): brand digital twin simulating campaigns pre-spend;
multimodal generation; GraphRAG audience graph; real-time creative/budget
optimizer. Target KPIs: CTR +20–35%, CAC −15–25%, time-to-launch −70%.

### Track 3 — Healthcare (HealthTech)
1. Maternal Health Companion (WHO/DGHS-aligned, GraphRAG)
2. Health Worker Assistant (field decision support, local LLM)
3. Telehealth Offline System (low-connectivity)
4. Nutrition AI Engine (personalized diet, localized)
5. Risk Prediction Engine (early complication detection, explainable)
6. Custom (must include ethics)

Example ("MaaCare AI"): WHO/DGHS knowledge graph + explainable outputs;
offline-first with SMS/IVR fallback; early-warning risk engine (anemia,
hypertension); health-worker copilot for triage/visit summaries.

### Track 4 — Online Commerce (E-Commerce)
1. Demand Forecasting AI (ML)
2. Dynamic Pricing Engine (elasticity-aware)
3. Recommendation System (GraphRAG, personalized shopping)
4. SME Dashboard (analytics, simple UX)
5. Integration Platform (payment/logistics APIs)
6. Custom (must show business value/monetization)

Example ("SMEBoost AI"): time-series + RL demand/pricing engine; graph-based
recommendation; SME action dashboard (restock/price/promo); bKash/Shopify/
Daraz plug-and-play APIs. Target KPIs: revenue +15–30%, stockouts −25%.

### Track 5 — Social Media (InfoTech)
1. Fact-Checking Engine (GraphRAG, trusted sources)
2. Deepfake Detection AI (vision models)
3. Misinformation Filter (NLP, explainable)
4. Sentiment Intelligence (trend-focused)
5. Trust Scoring System (GraphDB, bias-mitigated)
6. Custom (must address trust/ethical AI)

Example ("TruthLens AI"): RAG-based fact verification with citations;
vision-model deepfake + watermark detection; graph-based cross-source trust
scoring; public real-time dashboard with explainability.

### Final unified requirement (all tracks)

All teams must demonstrate: AI-native architecture (not surface-level AI),
full-stack integration (Lovable + Cursor + Claude Code + LLMs), RAG +
graph-based reasoning, real-world data via scraping/parsing, a
personalization engine, Bangla/localization support, and a scalable,
cloud-ready design.
